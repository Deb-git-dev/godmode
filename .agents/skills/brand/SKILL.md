# Brand Identity Governance Skill

## Tone and Voice
- **Direct & Authoritative**: Confident, technical, clear, devoid of empty corporate hype.
- **Architectural Grounding**: Every feature claim backed by verifiable facts and explicit provenance.

## Typographic Identity
- Primary Headline: Space Grotesk (technical, modernist, geometric).
- Body Copy: Inter (legible, neutral, high-density).
- System Code & Data: JetBrains Mono (precision monospace).
